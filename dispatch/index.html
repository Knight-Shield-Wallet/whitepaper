Dispatch

Governed AI work, routed to proof.

From request to receipt. Dispatch is being built to coordinate work while keeping authority, progress, and completion evidence explicit.

RequestTell the system what needs doing in normal language.
RouteWork is assigned to an appropriate capability.
TrackProgress and blockers remain visible.
ProveCompletion requires evidence rather than a self-declared “done.”

Important actions stay bounded.

Dispatch is designed so authority matters. Work should not silently expand into spending, publishing, account changes, legal commitments, or other sensitive actions without the permissions those actions require.
