# KSD Habitat brand kit

This kit captures the Habitat identity shown in the supplied reference: the Ht. app icon, the Habitat wordmark, and the privacy-first visual language.

## NV²D Protocol

**No visual or verbal diarrhea.** The superscript ² means one standard covers both.

- Lead with one direct product description that states the customer's job and outcome.
- Keep supporting copy only when it adds a concrete use case, status, privacy, or safety fact.
- Remove repeated claims, abstract slogans, throat-clearing, decorative clutter, and repeated labels.
- Put one clear product or benefit at the center of each visual.
- Follow the locked Habitat direction: warm ivory, oversized black sans-serif type, teal details, rounded Bento cards with black outlines, and semantic pastel fills.
- Do not invent a new palette or redraw the Ht icon. Keep Midnight Lens in its approved premium dark identity.

The product description carries the pitch.
## Brand promise

**Privacy should be normal.**  
**Share the proof. Keep the rest.**

Habitat should feel clear, calm, personal, and under the user's control. The interface puts permissions and evidence in view while keeping private material private.

## Typography

Use a heavy, modern sans serif for headlines and the wordmark. Prefer Inter when available, then the system sans-serif stack. Use compact line spacing and confident headline scale. Body copy stays readable and plain; avoid decorative serif headlines or thin display weights.

## Shape and layout

- Warm ivory canvas with generous open space.
- Large, left-aligned headlines with tight tracking.
- Rounded Bento cards, typically 20–24 px radius.
- Black 2 px outlines and a crisp 4 px black offset shadow on featured cards.
- Use the pastel palette to group product roles and make status easy to scan.
- Keep controls at least 44 px high and preserve visible keyboard focus.
- Use simple black line icons with consistent stroke weight.

## Component rules

- **Product cards:** pastel fill by product role, black outline, clear status label, short outcome-led title, expandable details where useful.
- **Primary actions:** black fill with white text and a firm black edge; secondary actions use ivory fill with black outline.
- **Status labels:** show the actual state in plain language. Color never substitutes for status text.
- **Workflow panels:** use colored stages with readable labels; keep approval, execution, verification, and evidence distinct.
- **Forms:** do not imply data is collected until the receiving service has been configured and tested.

## Product identity boundary

Habitat is the shared KSD interface and app identity. Keep product colors consistent with the tokens above. Midnight Lens retains its approved premium dark identity and dedicated visual treatment; do not flatten it into the pastel Habitat palette.
