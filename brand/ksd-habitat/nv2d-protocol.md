# NV²D Protocol — KSD company profile

**NV²D means No Visual or Verbal Diarrhea.** The squared 2 covers both the copy and the visual.

This protocol turns company facts and locked brand direction into concise advertising. It does not invent product capability, availability, evidence, or visual identity.

## Required inputs

Every advertising task must have these inputs before final output:

| Input | Required detail |
|---|---|
| Company and product | Legal company, public brand, exact product name |
| Audience and need | Who the message is for and the task or pain they recognize |
| Product truth | What it does, what it does not do, and current availability |
| Evidence and claims | What is implemented, tested, runtime-verified, approved, and safe to claim |
| Channel and goal | Placement, format or dimensions, and the one action the audience can take |
| Locked brand direction | Approved logo, palette, typography, layout, product-specific visual identity |
| Privacy and safety limits | Data handling, permissions, excluded claims, legal disclosures, sensitive information |
| Approval owner | Who approves the copy, image, and publication |

If a required input is missing, mark it **BLOCKED** or ask for that input. Do not fill gaps with guesses. A live action, contact path, price, feature, or launch claim requires its own evidence.

## KSD Labs profile

- **Company:** Knight Shield Digital Inc.
- **Public brand:** KSD Labs, the company’s research and product-development brand.
- **Audience:** People who want useful digital work done without losing control of permissions, privacy, or results.
- **Core promise:** Clear scope, human approval where required, and evidence people can inspect.
- **Copy rule:** Lead with one direct product description that states the customer’s job and outcome. Keep only details that add a concrete use case, status, privacy, or safety fact.
- **Visual rule:** Use the locked Habitat direction: warm ivory, oversized black sans-serif type, teal details, rounded black-outlined Bento cards, and semantic pastel product colors.
- **Product identity:** Keep Midnight Lens in its premium dark identity. The Ht mark is the Habitat app icon; the KSD Labs wordmark remains the public company-site identity.
- **Truth rule:** A concept, source build, demo, passing test, and verified runtime are different states. Use the state recorded in current source and proof; do not imply the product is live because a page describes it.
- **Privacy rule:** Do not publish private founder contact information. Do not imply a contact channel, signup, or service receives data unless that path has been configured and tested.
- **No-go:** No invented feature, pricing, availability, customer result, integration, proof, or visual depiction of an unverified runtime.

## Output limits

- Headline: one clear promise, preferably six words or fewer.
- Product description: one sentence, 30 words or fewer.
- Supporting line: optional; include only evidence, a concrete use case, status, privacy, or safety information.
- Call to action: one action; include it only when the action works.
- Visual: one product or benefit per frame. Use only approved product colors, assets, and identities. Remove decorative elements and duplicate labels.

## Review gate

Before delivery, confirm that:

1. The customer can say what the product does after reading the description.
2. Every claim matches the current product status and retained evidence.
3. The visual follows the locked Habitat or product-specific brand.
4. No phrase or visual repeats information without helping a decision.
5. Privacy, safety, accessibility, and approval requirements remain clear.

If any check fails, revise or mark the item **BLOCKED**. Publication still requires its normal independent review and release approval.
