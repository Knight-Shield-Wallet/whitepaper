Storage boundary

Your content is not assumed to live with KSD.

The default storage model is ON-DEVICE / USER CLOUD. KSD does not imply central hosting merely because a person uses Lens.

KSD-managed storage would be separate.

Any future KSD-managed storage is a distinct, explicit service with its own consent, limits, retention rules and commercial terms. Visual continuity does not merge storage or access authority.
