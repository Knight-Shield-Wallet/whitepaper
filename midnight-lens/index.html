Midnight Lens

One tap. One purpose. Temporary access.

Midnight Lens is being built as a practical trust and document layer for everyday life—not just a camera app.

Quick Session

The default interaction is intentionally small: choose what the other person can do, identify them as Guest or by KSD @name when needed, and start the temporary session.

1. Choose the actionFor example: Take Photos.
2. Guest or @nameGuest should work for basic use without requiring an account or app install.
3. Start SessionAccess stays narrow, purpose-bound, and temporary.
Useful paperwork

Plain-language agreements for ordinary situations.

Lens is intended to help people create practical documents without turning every task into a legal-document project.

Everyday agreements

Roommate agreements, vehicle sales, simple service contracts, NDAs, loan/payment agreements, equipment rentals, waivers, and similar documents.

Signing

Practical electronic-signing workflows combined with KSD-native proof and identity context where appropriate.

Selective disclosure

Share only the fact needed—such as identity verified, over 18, income threshold met, or credential verified—without exposing unnecessary source documents.

Midnight Lens is not legal advice. Generic agreements must be distinguished from official forms, regulated paperwork, and situations that require professional legal review.
