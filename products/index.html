Each product keeps a clear purpose and authority boundary. Shared KSD capabilities connect them where useful without turning every surface into the same app.

In development

Secretary

Less admin. More life.

Peel back for more info

Designed to help with inboxes, forms, applications, meeting preparation and reminders while carrying your preferences and context forward. Consequential actions come to you for approval. Your preferences stay with her. Your authority stays with you.

For example: Prepare an application using information you have approved, highlight what is missing, and bring the completed draft to you before submission.

In development

CafAI

Your AI team. One place to get work done.

Peel back for more info

Designed to bring your chosen AI tools together around one task, with shared context, clear responsibilities and approvals in one place. Compare independent ideas, follow the work and inspect the evidence behind a result.

For example: Ask several AI tools to evaluate a business idea, compare their findings, then approve a bounded next step.

In development

Dispatch

From a request to accountable work.

Peel back for more info

Designed to coordinate approved tasks, keep workers within their permissions and retain a record of actions and results. You can see what was authorized, what happened and what still needs attention.

For example: Approve a website update with a clear scope and receive a record of the changes and checks.

In development

Orbit

Confidence needs a second look.

Peel back for more info

Designed to independently examine evidence, identify gaps and distinguish a proven result from a completion claim. The worker doing the job is not its own final judge.

For example: Check that an updated website actually loads and works, rather than accepting a successful build as proof of delivery.
