Privacy should make life easier

The platform is designed around a simple rule: collect less, share less, and prove only what is needed for the task.

Dignity-first referrals

A resident can ask for food, fuel, transportation or other help. With consent, an authorized staff member can send a verified referral to an approved partner without forcing the resident to retell their entire situation.

Partner protection

Partners can see whether materially similar assistance was recently fulfilled where consent and program rules allow, without gaining broad access to resident information.

AI office support, not decision authority

AI can warn, route and summarize. It cannot decide tenancy, punish residents, deny support, or make disciplinary decisions.

Hard pilot boundary

No medical records, diagnoses, medications, VAC claim files, banking credentials, unrestricted financial records or clinical communications in the initial pilot.

Interactive demo

Switch roles to see how the same platform changes for a resident, staff member, administrator, owner or service partner.
