AI Workforce

Build a digital office around the work.

Instead of one generic assistant, KSD is building role-based workers with specific jobs, authority limits, and compatible skills.

Secretary

Scheduling, intake, communication and day-to-day administrative support.

Manager

Coordinates assignments, progress, blockers and next actions across the team.

Runner

Handles bounded execution work across approved systems and workflows.

Social Media Manager

Plans platform-specific posts from approved operational context, current events and brand rules.

Media Manager

Creates and selects social images and short-form media while enforcing KSD branding.

More specialist workers

Research, building, Android execution and other roles can be added without turning every worker into everything.

These workers are in development and are not represented as generally available for sale until their release gates pass.

How KSD thinks about AI

Make the system carry the complexity.

People should be able to state the outcome they want. The system should handle routing, boundaries, verification, and the next clear action.

Ask normallyUse plain language instead of memorizing system commands.
Keep authority boundedImportant actions stay inside explicit permissions.
Require evidenceCompletion should mean more than an AI saying “done.”
Show what mattersSurface the next useful action, not internal machinery.
