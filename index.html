Governing architecture

Permissions in. Proof out.

Governance means clear limits on what AI may do. Proof means evidence you can inspect. KSD is building both into the work so you spend less energy supervising and guessing.

Dispatch

Owns mission state, authority, assignment and governed execution. Workers act inside bounded missions; they do not become the control plane.

Orbit

Independently checks the evidence, challenges unsupported success claims and cannot mutate Dispatch state. Verification and execution stay structurally separate.
