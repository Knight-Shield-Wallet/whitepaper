## Next Gate

The next required gate is real two-phone proof:

1. Use an owner phone to create an opaque expiring invite from `/invite`.
2. Use a separate guest phone to accept the invite and declare consent.
3. Complete exactly one bounded synthetic action.
4. Capture denial evidence for revoke, expiry, and replay.
5. Retain the independent receipt with device context.

## Boundary

The included source gate is deterministic and useful, but it is not a substitute for the physical two-phone run. Do not mark live two-phone proof, media transport, identity verification, signing, or independent runtime proof as complete from this backpack alone.
