Digital office

Workers that fit together without becoming one giant assistant.

A business can combine roles such as Secretary, Manager, Runner, Researcher and media workers while Dispatch keeps assignments, authority and proof visible. Orbit provides independent supervision where the workflow requires it.

One role. One responsibility.

Workers should stay inside their assigned duties. A Secretary should not quietly become the employee doing the employee's job, and a media worker should not publish outside its authority.
