How it works

Connect → Prove → Share

01 / Connect

Private until you connect.

A request arrives in plain English. Lens helps you inspect what is actually being asked before you answer.

02 / Prove

Prove the fact. Keep the details private.

The Margin creates one deliberate pause where you choose the minimum information needed and withhold the rest.

03 / Share

Share only what matters.

Lens prepares the minimum response and can record the decision without unnecessarily copying personal values.
