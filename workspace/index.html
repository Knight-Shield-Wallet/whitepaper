KSD Workspace
In development

One governed place to work.

KSD Workspace is the human-facing operating environment for KSD. It is designed to bring approved tools, context, communication, rooms, mission state and proof into one protected workspace while keeping the underlying authority boundaries intact.

The key distinction

Workspace is where you work. Dispatch is what governs the work.

Workspace is not the control plane. A clean interface must not blur authority. Workspace can collect intent, show assignments, display proof and present human checkpoints. It does not independently issue mission authority, certify execution or change governed mission state.

System flow

From a human request to independently verified proof.

Identity, privacy & device boundary

Losing a phone should not mean losing the company.

The Workspace direction treats the app as a governed security perimeter rather than a copy of the full company. Authentication is designed around strong device/passkey controls, while sensitive knowledge and authority remain bounded instead of being permanently replicated onto the device.

Midnight Lens keeps its own job.

When identity, consent, signing, temporary access or selective disclosure is required, Workspace can invoke Midnight Lens without absorbing Lens authority. Detection or presentation alone never authorizes execution.

Data boundary

User-owned storage remains the default.

Workspace does not imply that KSD centrally stores a person's content. The default direction remains on-device or user-controlled cloud storage. Any KSD-managed storage would be a separate explicit service with its own consent, limits and retention terms.
