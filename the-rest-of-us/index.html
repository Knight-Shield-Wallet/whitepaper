Mission 1 · Proposed programs

Help a housing disruption stay temporary.

A failed move, a deposit gap, belongings in storage and paperwork can become one overwhelming problem. Our first mission connects housing stability, renters, landlords and homelessness prevention.

Close the whole gap.

Explore coordinated moving, storage, basic furniture, deposits, housing search and landlord matching. The question is what would actually stabilize the person.

Make room to recover.

The transition-home concept explores furnished short-term housing with a standard 90-day target and a tightly controlled extension where justified. This is a proposal, not an available housing service or placement offer.

Do not throw a symbolic amount of help at a full-size problem.

Help should be measured by the outcome achieved. A proposed Help Receipt records the agreed problem, approved support, evidence and remaining gaps. Public reporting should protect the person’s private information.

CONCEPT — PROPOSED, NOT YET BUILT

TRU Housing Score

Prove reliability without surrendering your whole financial life.

The proposed Rental Score Starter is an optional, private readiness score built from verified housing-related evidence you choose to add. It summarizes evidence; it does not replace that evidence. Nothing is shared with a housing provider until you approve it.

The old way

Hand over broad credit reports, bank statements and personal history, then hope unrelated information does not shape the decision.

The proposed way

Approve a narrow Rental Reliability Proof: relevant facts about rent-payment history, tenancy duration, the stated rent requirement and verified outstanding rental balances.

Peel back for more info

Your proof. Your choice.

  • Inspect each factor and its supporting evidence.
  • Correct, add or remove evidence before sharing.
  • Approve which relevant facts leave your hands.
  • No protected personal characteristics or unrelated private-life information as scoring inputs.
  • No hidden tenant risk score, tenant blacklist or automatic approval or denial.

KSD proposes Midnight technology where technically supported, with Midnight Lens as the request, consent and selective-disclosure interface. This is not a completed Midnight rental product or a claim of Midnight endorsement.

No scoring formula, live credential service, housing-provider acceptance or real applicant score is established by this page.
