The Rest of Us · Useful guide

What does an AI saying “done” actually tell you?

It tells you the AI has made a claim. To know whether the job succeeded, look for something you can inspect: the saved file, the sent message, the working page or a record of the action.

Start with the result you wanted

If you asked for a website, a successful build is one step. The useful result is a page you can open, with working links and the right content. Different steps need different evidence.

Keep permission separate from ability

An AI may be able to submit a form or change a website. That does not mean you have approved every submission or change. Clear scope makes it easier to delegate without losing control.

Give important work a second look

Ask what was checked, what failed and what remains uncertain. For consequential work, a separate check can catch mistakes the original worker missed. Several AI tools agreeing is not, by itself, evidence that something happened.

This is the approach KSD is building toward through Dispatch and Orbit. Their operational availability is a separate question from this design principle.
